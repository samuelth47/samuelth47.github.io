## Software engineer building reliable, useful systems

I spent three years at Bank of America as a Software Engineer II, where I led a cross-team effort to automate full VMware cluster builds with Ansible, cutting manual provisioning and human error. I enjoy turning manual, error-prone processes into dependable software.
